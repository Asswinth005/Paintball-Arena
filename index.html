Safety & Rules

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about gear, safety, age limits, and booking.

We provide industry-standard full-face masks (thermal or anti-fog), chest/neck protectors, and gloves with every rental package. Our markers are chronographed daily to ensure safe muzzle velocity. You’re welcome to bring your own mask if it meets ASTM standards — our staff will inspect it. No outside paintballs allowed.

Players must be at least 10 years old. For ages 10–15, a parent or guardian must sign a waiver and remain on-site. Our low-impact paintball (caliber .50) is available for younger players, with lighter markers and reduced velocity. Ages 16+ can play standard .68 caliber with a signed waiver.

Wear long sleeves, long pants, and closed-toe shoes (sneakers or cleats). Loose, dark clothing is best. A paintball hit can sting for a second — that’s part of the rush! Our referees enforce surrender rules and safe distance to minimize close-range impacts. Most players say the adrenaline makes you forget the sting.

Weekends fill up 5–7 days in advance, especially for large groups. We recommend booking at least 48 hours ahead for weekday sessions and a week ahead for weekends. For birthday parties or corporate events, book 2–3 weeks out. Walk-ins are welcome if space allows, but we can’t guarantee marker availability.

Absolutely — outside food and non-alcoholic beverages are welcome in our staging area. We have picnic tables, shade canopies, and a food truck on weekends. No glass containers, and alcohol is strictly prohibited before or during play. We also offer snack packs and pizza delivery add-ons for groups.
