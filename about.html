Our Mission

Real tactics. Real adrenaline. Zero compromise.

We built Crossfire for players who want more than a casual afternoon. Our zones are designed around real combat principles — cover, communication, and objective control. Every bunker, every sightline, and every mission is engineered to make you think, move, and play as a squad.

But adrenaline means nothing without safety. That's why we invest in pro-grade gear, daily chronograph checks, and on-field referees who know the game. You play hard. We keep it fair.

The Journey

From one field to a battle complex

Six years of building, expanding, and listening to our players.

2019

The First Field

Crossfire opens with a single speedball field and a rental shed. Ten markers, two referees, and a whole lot of ambition.

2020

Woodland Expansion

We acquire five acres of forest and open the Woodland Zone — complete with natural cover, bunkers, and a stream.

2021

Urban Combat Opens

Our CQB mock city goes live — multi-level buildings, alleyways, and the toughest tactical gameplay in the region.

2022

Pro Shop & Armory

We launch our on-site pro shop and upgrade our rental fleet to tournament-grade markers. Real-time scoring is introduced.

2023

Night Ops Launches

Tracer rounds, LED markers, and floodlit combat. Night Ops becomes our most requested (and most limited) experience.

2024

Tournament Hosting

Crossfire hosts its first regional qualifier. We add spectator seating, a PA system, and full tournament infrastructure.

2025

The Player Dashboard

We launch our digital player portal — track your stats, book sessions, and join the Crossfire community online.

What We Stand For

The Crossfire code

Four principles that guide every decision we make — from field design to referee training.

Safety Without Compromise

ASTM-certified masks, daily chrono checks, and referees on every field. We never cut corners on protection.

Tactical Authenticity

Our fields are designed with real combat geometry in mind. Cover matters. Communication wins. Every game is a real challenge.

Squad Over Solo

Paintball is a team sport. We build private staging areas, group packages, and missions that require real teamwork.

Community First

From first-timers to tournament veterans, everyone gets the same respect. We listen to our players and build what they ask for.
